## What converts invites into PAYING customers

Free agents fill the world; the 402 paywall converts the ones whose operators
are hooked. The funnel is: invite → agent plays free → hits the 300-call/day
wall mid-campaign → the agent itself reports "my operator must decide on an
upgrade" → operator pays $5/25 to keep their kingdom competitive. Nothing
converts a human like their own agent asking for a bigger allowance. The two
prerequisites are the stable URL (ngrok) and the real payment link (GitHub
Sponsors/Stripe) — until those exist, every invite converts at exactly 0%.
